Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Spendly Pro. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly Pro handles data, including your local database and Google Drive integration.

2. Data Collection & Privacy by Design

Spendly Pro is a local-first personal finance manager. This means:

  • No server-side databases: We do not host or store your financial transactions, account credentials, or budget configurations on any external servers owned by us.
  • Local Browser Storage: Your data is stored directly inside your browser's local cache (localStorage).

3. Google Drive Integration (AppData)

If you choose to sync your database with Google Drive:

  • Spendly Pro requests access strictly to the Google Drive AppData folder (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata).
  • This scope is restricted to a hidden folder created specifically for Spendly Pro. The app cannot read, write, or access any other files, folders, or documents stored inside your personal Google Drive.
  • The sync files (e.g., spendly_db.json) are stored within your own private Google storage quota.

4. Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

Because we do not collect your financial data, we have zero capability to sell, rent, share, or disclose your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Deletion

You can erase all local data from the app at any time by going to the Profile tab and selecting "Reset Workspace". If connected, you can also disconnect Google Drive and manage your file storage directly within your Google Account settings.
